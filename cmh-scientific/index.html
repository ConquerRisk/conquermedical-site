CMH Scientific · R&D laboratory

Scientific instruments for biomedical decisions.

CMH Scientific develops and validates the computational methods behind Conquer Medical Health's clinical, therapeutic-development, trial-design, research and measurement products. The public layer contains browser demonstrations and technical reports; protected production engines remain behind authenticated services.

The instrument suite

Each instrument answers a different question. They can operate independently or as parts of a larger scientific workflow.

Patient-specific intervention

ABP · AI as a Biological Primitive

Builds a model from an individual's own observed responses and interrogates it in reverse for supported intervention directions.

Open demo →
Selective clinical assistance

Deferra

Separates inadequate measurement from trajectory departure and changed intervention-response relation, then decides when AI should assist, defer or withhold.

Open surgery demo →
Structural memory

TotemicAI

Compiles sparse structural landmarks at trajectory-departure points. The selection policy can be specified a priori or learned from training data.

Open demo →
Information sufficiency

NPIS

Tests whether the observed substrate contains the distinctions required by a declared task before model selection.

Open demo →
Recoverability & acquisition

OptiCeil

Distinguishes measurement limitation from observer limitation and inverts the recoverability frontier into an acquisition prescription.

Open demo →
Adaptive measurement

Acquire

Chooses what to measure next, in whom, and when further acquisition stops earning its cost.

Open demo →
Falsification

CTF · Clinical Trial Falsifier

Tests whether apparent responder or harm subgroups survive the identical procedure in negative-control patients.

Open demo →
Scientific claim adjudication

AIO · AI On…

Reruns a field's claims on a common substrate and protocol to determine what reproduces and what can actually be distinguished.

Open demo →
Scientific control

TopolAI

Returns an action rather than another score: use, continue, selectively acquire, change measurement, add modality, reacquire or abstain.
